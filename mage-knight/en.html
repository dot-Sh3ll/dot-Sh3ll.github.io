Reference

The essential rules on one page, to keep open while you play.

Your turn

  1. Move (optional), as many spaces as your Move points allow.
  2. Take a single action: fight or interact with a site. After the action, no more movement.
  3. End of turn: discard the cards you played, then draw up to your Hand Limit.

Instead, you may Rest: discard one non-Wound card and as many Wounds as you like (Standard Rest), or a single Wound if that is all you have in hand (Slow Recovery). Every turn, you must play or discard at least one card.

The deck is only reshuffled at the end of the Round. When it is empty at the start of your turn, you may announce the end of the Round; with an empty deck and an empty hand, you must announce it. Day turns to night, and vice versa.

Playing a card

  • Basic effect: free.
  • Stronger effect: pay one mana of the card's colour.
  • Sideways (turned horizontally): any card is worth Move 1, Influence 1, Attack 1 or Block 1.
  • A Wound can't be played. It takes up a slot in your hand.

Mana

  • Source: one die per turn, rerolled at the end of your turn.
  • Tokens: vanish at the end of the turn.
  • Crystals: are kept, three per colour at most.
  • Gold: replaces any basic colour, by day only.
  • Black: by night only, for the stronger effect of Spells.

Combat in four phases

  1. Ranged and siege: only these attacks count. Against a fortified enemy, only siege counts.
  2. Block: match or exceed the enemy Attack, otherwise the Block is lost.
  3. Damage: a Unit absorbs its Armor and becomes Wounded; the hero takes one Wound per Armor step, rounded up.
  4. Attack: all your attacks count. You must reach the enemy's Armor in a single blow.

Enemy abilities

  • Fortified: immune to Ranged Attacks.
  • Swift: you need twice the Block.
  • Brutal: damage doubled if not blocked.
  • Poison: each Wound in hand adds another one to the discard pile.
  • Resistance: attacks of this type are halved.
  • Knocked out: as many Wounds as your Hand Limit in a single combat, and you discard the rest of your hand.

Sites

  • Village: recruit, heal for 3 Influence per point of Healing.
  • Orc Marauders: can be attacked from an adjacent space. Moving along their space provokes them.
  • Keep: entering it starts the assault. Fortified garrison, Reputation −1. Once conquered, it increases your hand nearby.
  • Monster Den: action to fight a monster; reward at the end of the turn, two Crystals rolled on the die.
  • Mine: ending your turn on it gives a crystal.
  • Magical Glade: a gold mana token (black at night) at the start of the turn, one Wound removed from your hand or discard pile at the end.

Fame and Reputation

  • The Fame comes from defeated enemies. Level thresholds: 3, 8, 15, 24.
  • Even levels: a Skill and an Advanced Action card. Odd levels: extra Armor, Hand Limit or Command token.
  • The Reputation modifies your Influence at each interaction. Defeating marauders raises it, attacking a keep lowers it.

Terms and limits

  • This tutorial uses the terms of the English rulebook. The French edition sometimes uses different names for the same things.
  • The board, enemies and Units in the hands-on cases are examples for learning; rely on the tiles, tokens and cards in your box.
  • In the combat cases, cards marked "example" are invented for the exercise. The others are the Basic Action cards.
  • The free game remains simplified: a single enemy per combat, no Spells, Artifacts, Skills or Tactics.

Game companion

Dummy player rules reminder
  • The Dummy player gets the 16 Basic Action cards of an unchosen Hero and 3 Crystals, matching the colours of the dots at the bottom of its Hero card. It may exceed 3 Crystals per colour.
  • On its turn: empty deck, it announces the end of the Round. Otherwise it discards 3 cards. If it owns Crystals of the last card's colour, it discards that many more, without looking at their colour.
  • If it can't discard all the planned cards, it discards what it can and announces the end of the Round on its next turn.
  • When a player announces the end of the Round, each of the others, Dummy included, plays one last turn.
  • In solo, you choose your Tactic first, then the Dummy takes one at random. Both are removed for the rest of the game. The lowest Tactic goes first.
  • In co-op, the Dummy draws its Tactic at random before the players. At the end of each Round, the players remove from the game one of the Tactics they used, except after the last Day and the last Night.
  • At each new Round: the Advanced Action removed from the bottom of the Offer goes into the Dummy's Deck, which is reshuffled. The Spell removed from the Offer goes under its deck as usual, and the Dummy gains a Crystal of its colour.
