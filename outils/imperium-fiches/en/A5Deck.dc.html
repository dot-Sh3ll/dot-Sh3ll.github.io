Imperium: Classics

Deck, Market and game end

Sheet 1 · back

Empty deck: you shuffle

Deck empty when you must Draw: before shuffling, once per turn, add a card to your discard pile, with a free Exhaust token as a marker:

  1. Nation deck: its top card. If it is the Accession, flip your State card: you are now an Empire. Vikings: their Zenith flips nothing, it triggers the end of the game.
  2. Nation deck empty: pay the cost of a Development card of your choice. Developing the last one triggers the Countdown.

No free token: shuffle without adding. “If possible” never makes you shuffle; “up to X” shuffles if needed.

The Market: 5 cards, always refilled at once

Slots: Main · Main · Civilised · Tradition · Region. One Unrest under each card, except Regions.

VerbYou takeThe Unrest under it
AcquireThe visible card and its resources.You take it too.
InnovateThe visible card and its resources, or the top face-down card of the Region, Tradition or Civilised deck.Goes back to the pile.

Tributary or empty deck: search the Main deck. Nothing found: 2 Progress.

Getting rid of Unrest

  • Play it: 1 Action, then 3 Materials, or 1 Population, or discard 2 cards.
  • Pacify: all of those in your hand, for free. “Return an Unrest”: no cost.

Countdown

Triggered by: Main deck empty; last card Developed; King of Kings flipped face down; a card effect.

Finish the round (Solstice included), then play one full round. Score: the most points wins.

Collapse

The Unrest pile runs out: the game ends at once, even during a Countdown.

The fewest Unrest wins (hand, play, deck, discard, History). Tie: points decide.

What scores

Yes: hand, play area, deck, discard pile, History, Power card, Reserved cards, 1 point per Progress in your reserve. No: Nation deck, undeveloped Developments, Progress placed on cards. Star card: 10 points at most.
