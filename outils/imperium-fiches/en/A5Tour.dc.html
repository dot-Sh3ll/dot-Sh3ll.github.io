Imperium: Classics

Your turn

Sheet 1 · front

The round

The player to the left of the Solstice token starts, then play goes clockwise. On your turn: one single order, then your Cleanup. Once everyone has played: Solstice.

Activation

Almost every turn.

Use your 3 Action tokens and 5 Exhaust tokens in any order you like.

Research

One card, without Unrest.

  1. Discard your whole hand.
  2. Innovate a Region, Tradition, Civilised or Tributary (no Fame).

Pacify

The big clean-up.

Return as many Unrest cards from your hand to the pile as you like, without paying anything.

Research and Pacify: no Action and no Exhaust ability. Passive abilities stay active.

Actions and Exhaust abilities

  • Action: remove an Action token from your State card, usually to play a card from your hand. It resolves, then goes to your discard pile, unless it is a permanent card (it stays in play).
  • Exhaust: place an Exhaust token on a card in play (or your Power card) that has none, then resolve the text. Once per turn per card. Putting the card down does not trigger its ability.
  • Free action: no Action token needed, once per turn for the same card. Written costs are still due.
  • Gain an Action: take a token from the supply, even beyond 3.

Cleanup

  1. 1 Progress from the supply (not your reserve) onto a Market card.
  2. Take back your tokens: 3 Actions, 5 Exhaust.
  3. Discard as many cards from your hand as you like.
  4. Draw up to 5. Already 5 or more: you don't draw, and you keep them all.

After step 1, the next player may already start.

Paying

1 Progress = 1 Population
1 Progress = 2 Materials

Never the other way round, and no change given. Only your reserve pays: a resource on a card cannot be spent.

Solstice

Everyone resolves all their Solstice effects (play area, Power card, State card) at the same time, in the order of their choice. No Action, no Exhaust.
