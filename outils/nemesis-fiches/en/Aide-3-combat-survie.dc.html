Nemesis: Retaliation

Combat and survival

Sheet 2 · front

Combat

  • Shoot (Room): +1 Hit, then Shoot die. Critical = dead; 2 to 5 = dead if ≤ Hits; Ammo face = spend Ammo.
  • Melee: +1 Contamination, +1 Hit, Shoot die (last face: nothing). If the Intruder survives, it strikes back.
  • Burst (Corridor): spend Ammo, 1 to 4 Hits: at most 1 per Adult or Larva, exactly 2 per Drone, the Queen as many as you like.
  • Grenade: Burst die + 2 Hits in an adjacent Corridor (not a Burst).
  • Larva: 1 Hit kills it. Its Attack: +1 Contamination and the Larva on your board.
  • Queen: Hit track; at the end, a Health card (discard as many cards as its number, apply the effect, marker to 0).

Escaping

  • Lander: when the Round track reaches its token, reveal the top Anti-Aircraft token. Active: it is shot down. Inactive: it lands in the Landing Zone.
  • Boarding: Use the Landing Zone, with a Noise roll. On board: your turn is skipped, no Oxygen or Health lost. An Intruder appears there: everyone gets off.
  • Launch: at the start of the Event Phase, any passenger may decide it alone. Everyone on board has escaped.
  • Escape Shuttle: Noise roll, one seat only; Anti-Aircraft does not stop it. Hibernatorium: Noise roll, if it has been activated.
  • Autodestruction: token 5 Rounds after the current one. When reached, the Facility explodes: everyone inside dies, Hibernatorium included. Only the Reactor stops it.

Health and Oxygen

  • Healthy → Injured → Heavily Injured → skull. Armor: destroyed instead of losing 1 point.
  • Oxygen 7 at most; Oxygen token +3, Medpack +2 Health.
  • Counter below 0: Suffocating, one turn to find Oxygen.

End of the game

  1. No Larva: all your cards in hand, Infection Procedure.
  2. With a Larva: +1 Contamination, hatching (4 cards; one Contamination = death).
  3. Alive, escaped or hibernating: reveal your Objective.

Nightmare Rule: when in doubt, apply the worst outcome for the players, then for the first in Turn order. Local effects: with no clear target, the effect applies in your Room.
