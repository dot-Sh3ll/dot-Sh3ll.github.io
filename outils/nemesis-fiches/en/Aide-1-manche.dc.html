Nemesis: Retaliation

A Round

Sheet 1 · front

1. Player Phase

In turn, until everyone has Passed: 2 Actions, then −1 Oxygen if your Section's Life Support is inactive, then −1 Health if your Room has Fire.

Objective: choose it before, between or after your 2 Actions, never in the middle of one (3 / 2 / 2 / 1 / 1 cards).

2. Intruder Phase

1 Hit to each Intruder in a Room with Fire (burning Nest: −1 Egg).

Then Intruders Attack, Room by Room from the top left, largest first, on the first in Turn order.

3. Event Phase

Lander passengers: launch possible at the start of the phase.

Event card (movement, main effect, secondary effect), then 1 token from the Intruder bag.

4. Cleanup Phase

Starting Player token to the left; everyone draws up to 5 cards; Round track +1.

Lander on space 10: Anti-Aircraft check. Autodestruction: explosion. After Round 14: end.
