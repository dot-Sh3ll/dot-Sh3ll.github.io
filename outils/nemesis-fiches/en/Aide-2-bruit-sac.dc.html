Noise, Intruders and Items

Sheet 1 · back

Noise roll

  • 1 to 4: for each adjacent Corridor with that number, an Intruder there → the largest enters and Attacks; a Noise marker → resolve it (bag, Corridor column); nothing → place a Noise marker.
  • Hazard: a token from the bag, Room column: the Intruder shown appears in your Room.
  • Reinforced Corridor = 0. Full Corridor = 6 Intruders (Queen = 4) or 1 Noise marker.

Items

  • Search: 1 Item card per Item icon of the Room; keep 1, put the others back under their deck.
  • Regular Items: in your Backpack, no limit, secret. Heavy Items (Weapons, Eggs): one per hand, 2 hands.
  • Interaction: with their consent, an Item can be used on a Character in your Room (Health, Serious Wound, Malfunction, Oxygen). Otherwise, Trade (1 card, Not in Combat).
