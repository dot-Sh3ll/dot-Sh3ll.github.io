Moving

  • Add up your Movement points and spend them space by space, as many spaces as you like.
  • Never onto a rampaging enemy's space.
  • Unconquered fortified site: entering it = assault, end of Movement, −1 Reputation.
  • You cannot move any more after your Action.

Provoking a rampaging enemy

The combat happens at once and becomes your Action. Both adjacent enemies, before and after the step, attack together. You can also challenge an adjacent enemy, as an Action, at the end of your Movement.

Exploring

From a space on the edge of the map, spend 2 Movement: reveal the top tile of the stack and place it against that edge. No need to enter it: you can keep moving. Place the Enemy tokens of its sites (face down on fortified sites).

Influence and Reputation

  • Recruiting, healing, buying: you pay in Influence (cards, sideways effects).
  • Your Reputation adds or subtracts its bonus once per interaction, never in combat.
  • “×” space: no more interaction possible.
  • Reputation: assault −1, plunder −1, burned Monastery −3. Orcs +1, Draconum +2.

Day

Gold die = joker, black die unusable. Garrisons revealed when you are adjacent.

Night

Gold die unusable, black mana usable. Garrisons revealed only on assault.
