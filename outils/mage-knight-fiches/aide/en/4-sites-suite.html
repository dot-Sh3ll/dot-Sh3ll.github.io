Adventure and Cities

Adventure sites
Entering is your Action.
Ancient Ruins
  • Token face down at Night, revealed by Day or when a player enters.
  • Altar: 3 mana shown on the token → reward: 7 Fame
  • Enemies: fight them, gain the token's reward
  • Undefeated: they stay
  • If conquered: 1 Shield
Monster Den
  • 1 brown enemy drawn on entry.
  • If conquered: 1 Shield + 2 Crystals
  • Undefeated: it stays
Spawning Grounds
  • 2 brown enemies drawn on entry.
  • If conquered: 1 Shield + 1 Artifact + 3 Crystals
Dungeon
  • 1 brown enemy drawn on entry.
  • Night rules · no Units
  • If conquered: 1 Shield + die: black/gold = 1 Spell, otherwise 1 Artifact
Tomb
  • 1 red enemy drawn on entry.
  • Night rules · no Units
  • If conquered: 1 Shield + 1 Spell + 1 Artifact
Cities · fortified site
Assault: move in at the terrain cost, −1 Reputation per player, Fortified defenders.
  • Defenders face down on the City card, revealed when a player is adjacent, even at Night.
  • Fortified defenders + color bonus
  • 1 Shield per defender defeated
  • All defeated: conquered; Lord = most Shields
Conquered
  • Anyone can be there, no combat between players
  • Recruit (City icon) and its interaction
  • Interaction here: +1 Influence per Shield
  • End of turn in it / next to it: hand +2 (Lord) or +1
