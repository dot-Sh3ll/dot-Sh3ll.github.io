The Lost Legion sites

Adventure sites
Entering is your Action.
Maze
  • 1 brown enemy drawn, discarded after the combat
  • If defeated: 1 Shield on the path + reward
Labyrinth
  • 1 red Draconum drawn, discarded after the combat
  • If defeated: 1 Shield on the path + reward + 1 Advanced Action
  • A single Unit (ready, unwounded) comes with you; no other Unit for the whole action
  • Choose a path: pay 2, 4 or 6 Movement (not the points left over from your Movement phase)
  • Reward: path 2 = 2 Crystals of your choice · 4 = 1 Spell · 6 = 1 Artifact

Partly conquered site: another player can enter by another path. A player conquers a given Maze or Labyrinth only once; each path can be conquered only once. Game end: 2 Fame per Shield placed (Adventurer title).

Sites without combat
Usable as soon as the tile is revealed.
Deep Mine
  • End your turn on it: 1 Crystal of a color of your choice among the Mine's colors
Refugee Camp
  • Recruit any Unit
  • Extra cost: Village +0 · Keep, Tower, Monastery +1 · City +3
  • Units recruitable at several sites: lowest extra cost
Walls
Fortified edge between two spaces.
Movement
  • Crossing: +1 Movement
  • Cost reductions do not change this
  • No effect on Flight and other effects
  • A rampaging enemy never attacks across them
Combat across them
  • Challenging a rampaging enemy: it is Fortified
  • Attacking a player or Volkare: they are Fortified
  • Assaulting a fortified site: defenders are Double Fortified
