Enemy abilities

Offensive

Fire Attack
Efficient Block: Ice or Cold Fire. Other Blocks ÷ 2.

Ice Attack
Efficient Block: Fire or Cold Fire. Other Blocks ÷ 2.

Cold Fire Attack
Efficient Block: Cold Fire only. Other Blocks ÷ 2

Summon
In the Block phase, a brown enemy is drawn to set the attack to block. It is then discarded.

Swift
To block it, you need 2 × its Attack.

Brutal
Unblocked: it deals 2 × its Attack as damage.

Poison
Unit: 2 Wounds.
Hero: for each Wound in hand, +1 Wound to the discard pile.

Paralyze
Wounded Unit = destroyed.
Wounded Hero = discards their non-Wound cards.

Assassination
Only the Hero can take the damage.

Cumbersome
In the Block phase, Movement counts as Block points.

Vampiric
For the rest of this combat
+1 Armor per Unit wounded or Wound in hand caused by its attack.

Defensive

Fortified
In the Ranged phase, only siege attacks count.

Physical Resistance
physical attacks ÷ 2 (sideways cards too).

Fire Resistance
Fire Attack ÷ 2. Immune to red non-Attack effects.

Ice Resistance
Ice Attack ÷ 2. Immune to blue non-Attack effects.

Fire and Ice
As above, and Cold Fire attacks are ÷ 2 too. Immune to red / blue non-Attack effects.

Elusive
Highest Armor value in the Ranged phase.
Lowest Armor value if all its attacks were blocked, otherwise the highest.

Unfortified
Ignores the site's fortifications.

Arcane Immunity
Only Attacks, Blocks and effects on its attack affect it.

Defend
When you attack an enemy, +X to its Armor (once per combat)

Multiple attacks

Several values · each one separately. Blocked if all of them are.
