Entering combat · it is your Action for the turn

  • Provoke or challenge a rampaging enemy · Assault a fortified site (−1 Reputation) · Enter an adventure site.
  • All the enemies engaged form a single combat.

The 4 phases

1 · Ranged and Siege

Kill them before they attack, with ranged or siege attacks only.
Fortified: siege attacks only.
Double Fortified: untouchable.

2 · Block

Each attack is blocked separately: Block ≥ Attack.
Watch out for elements and resistances.
Swift: you need 2 × the Attack.

3 · Damage

Unblocked attacks, one by one, on Units and/or Hero.

4 · Attack

All attack types can be used here, ranged and siege included. Group: sum of Armors, all Resistances combined.

Don't forget · Group: every Resistance of every enemy applies to the whole group. You choose freely whom to group. Tip: isolate resistant enemies.

Working out “½ inefficient”

  1. Add up all the inefficient values.
  2. Divide this total by 2, rounded down.
  3. Add the efficient values, in full.

Example: Ice Block 3 (efficient) + Physical Block 3 + Fire Block 2 (inefficient) against a Fire attack 6 gives 3 + (3 + 2) ÷ 2 = 3 + 2 = 5 < 6, the block fails.
