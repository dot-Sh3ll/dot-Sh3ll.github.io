Sites

Rampaging enemies
Provoked when you move from one adjacent space to another, or challenged as an Action.
You cannot move onto their space.
Orc Marauders
  • Green enemy, face up
  • Reward: +1 Reputation
Draconum
  • Red enemy, face up
  • Reward: +2 Reputation
Fortified sites
Assault: move in at the terrain cost, −1 Reputation, Fortified defender.
Keep
  • Grey enemy, face down
    Revealed by Day if a player is adjacent
  • If conquered: 1 Shield
  • Recruit (icon )
  • End of turn in it / next to it: hand limit +1
  • PvP: another player can attack it
Mage Tower
  • Violet enemy, face down
    Revealed by Day if a player is adjacent
  • If conquered: 1 Shield + 1 Spell
  • Then for everyone: recruit (icon )
  • Buy: 7 Influence + 1 mana → 1 Spell
Sites without combat
Village
  • Recruit (icon )
  • Heal: 3 Influence → 1 Healing
  • Plunder (on another player's turn):
    −1 Reputation, +2 cards
Monastery
  • Recruit (icon )
  • Heal: 2 Influence → 1 Healing
  • Training:
    6 Influence → 1 Advanced Action
  • Burn the Monastery:
    −3 Reputation, violet enemy
    Your Units do not take part
    Reward: 1 Shield + 1 Artifact
Crystal Mine
  • End your turn on it: 1 Crystal of its color
Magical Glade
  • Start of turn: gold mana (Day) / black mana (Night)
  • End of turn: destroy 1 Wound

Rewards received at the end of the turn · Crystal by die: gold = your choice, black = 1 Fame · Artifact: draw 2, keep 1 · ● = danger.
