Don't forget · Taking damage is always at least 1 Wound.
Exception · a Unit with the Resistance to the attack's type AND Armor ≥ the attack

Phase 3 · A Unit takes damage

  • Any ready or spent Unit can take damage as long as it is not already wounded.

  • If the Unit resists the type of damage taken AND its Armor is greater than or equal to the attack, it takes no Wound.

  • Otherwise, the Unit takes 1 Wound and subtracts its Armor from the damage; the rest must be taken by another Unit or the Hero.

    Paralyze : if wounded, it is destroyed.
    Poison: if wounded, it takes 2 Wounds.
    Assassination     : Units may not take this damage; only the Hero can.

Wounds go into your hand.

Poison: +1 Wound to your discard pile per Wound taken.
Paralyze: discard your non-Wound cards.

Knocked out: if the Wounds taken during this combat reach your hand limit, discard all your non-Wound cards. The combat goes on.

Dragon Attack 6 Fire, Brutal, against a Unit with Armor 4 and a Hero with Armor 3

Block: against Fire, only an Ice (or Cold Fire) Block is efficient. Ice Block 6: attack blocked. Physical Block 4 + Fire Block 4: both inefficient, (4 + 4) ÷ 2 = 4 < 6: attack not blocked.

Not blocked: 6 × 2 (Brutal) = 12 damage. The Unit absorbs 4 damage and takes 1 Wound: 8 remain. The Hero takes 8 ÷ 3 = 2.67, rounded up = 3 Wounds.
Result: the Unit is wounded, the Hero adds 3 Wounds to their hand.

After the combat

  • Fame: the value of each defeated enemy (Fame and level at the end of the turn).
  • Site conquered: place a Shield; reward at the end of the turn.
  • Undefeated enemies: they stay on their space. Failed assault: move back to your previous space.
  • Wounded Units : unusable until healed. Healing a Unit takes X Healing, X being the Unit's level, top left of its card.
