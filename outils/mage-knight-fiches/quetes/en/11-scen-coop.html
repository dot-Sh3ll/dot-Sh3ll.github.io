Homemade scenarios

Rulebook co-op setup: one Dummy player, who draws its Tactic at random before you. At the end of each Round, remove from the game one of the Tactics the players used. Remove the four interactive Spells (no. 17 to 20) and each Hero's interactive Skill. No combat between players.

Quest module required, in Normal mode by default (module rules, pages 1 to 8). The City levels given already account for the quests: no +1. A site offers quests only once for the whole team.

Brothers in Arms4 Rounds · 2 h 30

Two Knights, one quest board. Share out the villages, and lend each other a hand when a quest is too much for one.

Goal · complete 8 quests together, at least 3 each, 2 of them tier II.

Setup
  • Open board, starting tile side B.
  • 7 Countryside tiles: keep in priority those with a Village or Monastery.
  • 2 Core tiles without a City.
Scenario rules
  • No rules beyond the common setup.

End · as soon as the 8th quest is completed, if the goal is met, each player plays one last turn, not the Dummy. Otherwise, at the end of the 4th Round.

Guardians of the Realm5 Rounds · 3 h 30

The Realm is threatened on all sides. Take the City, then become the champions its people were waiting for.

Goal · conquer the City, then complete 2 tier III quests, one each.

Setup
  • Open board, starting tile side B.
  • 8 Countryside tiles.
  • 1 Core tile with a City and 2 without.
  • The City is level 6.
Scenario rules
  • Guardians: each Race completed gives +1 Reputation to both Heroes.

End · as soon as the 2nd tier III quest is completed, each player plays one last turn, not the Dummy. Otherwise, at the end of the 5th Round.
