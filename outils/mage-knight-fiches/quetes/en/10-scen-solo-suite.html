The Shadow Brotherhood5 Rounds · 3 h 30

Honest folk shut their doors on you, but the Brotherhood always has work for an unscrupulous Knight. Take a City and become its shadow agent.

Goal · end the game with a Reputation that gives you a penalty, after completing 3 ⚫ quests, one of them tier III.

Setup
  • Pyramid board.
  • 8 Countryside tiles.
  • 1 Core tile with a City and 1 without.
  • The revealed City is level 5.
Scenario rules
  • Shadow path: when a quest offers 😇 or 😈, you always choose 😈.
  • Shady contacts: while your Reputation gives you a penalty, each time you roll, you may change the result of one of the two rolls to ⚫.
  • Reminder: with a Reputation penalty, Monasteries are closed to you, and in a conquered City you may only accept a ⚫ quest.

End · as soon as the 3rd ⚫ quest is completed, if one is tier III, play one last turn. Otherwise, at the end of the 5th Round. Your Reputation must then give you a penalty.

Score: a win if the goal is met. To compare your games: your Fame plus Achievements without the titles, plus 5 points per tier I quest completed, 10 per tier II, 15 per tier III, and 30 per unused Round.
