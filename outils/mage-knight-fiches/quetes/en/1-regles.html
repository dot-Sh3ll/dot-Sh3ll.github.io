Checking and accepting a quest
  • During your turn, on a Village, a Monastery or a conquered City, you may check the quest board.
  • One active quest per player. A site offers quests only once per game.
  1. Roll a mana die (not one from the Source): quest A of its colour.
  2. Roll it again: quest B of its colour.
  3. Read the Day or Night page, depending on the Round.
  4. Choose one, or turn both down.
  5. Place a Shield token to show that you have already checked this site's quests.

Quest already done or impossible (site missing…): take the next one in the same column, in the order 🔴 🔵 🟢 ⚪ 🟡 ⚫, then 🔴 again.

For a harder game

Mercenary mode: a single roll, quest A only.

Heroic mode: a single roll, quest B only.

😈 Shadow brotherhood: if your Reputation gives you an Influence penalty, Monasteries are closed to you. In a conquered City, you may only accept a ⚫ quest. Shady contacts: each time you roll, you may change the result of one of the two rolls to ⚫.

Deadlines
  • ⚡ Immediate: right now, or it fails.
  • ⚔️ Combat: before the end of the Round, as your action for the turn (no interaction or assault that turn).
  • ⏳ End of Round: before the end of the Round.
Success

Place one of your Shield tokens on the site: +1 Influence for every interaction here.

Failure or refusal

Failure (deadline missed, enemy not defeated): −1 Reputation. Refusal: no penalty. Either way, place a Shield token of a Hero nobody plays: the site is exhausted.

“Greatest Knight-errant” title: the player who completed the most quests gains +3 Fame (tie: +1 each).
