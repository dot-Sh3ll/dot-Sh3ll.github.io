Advanced Actions, Spells and Artifacts come only from black quests, which are riskier.

Clarifications
  • Quest enemies: normal Fame, no Reputation unless stated. If not defeated, they are discarded and the quest fails.
  • Values outside combat (Block, Attack, Move, Influence, Heal): generated with your cards and Units as usual. They cannot be used for anything else that turn.
  • Reputation rewards: you may always take the Fame shown instead.
  • 😇 Hero or 😈 Rogue: some quests let you choose. The rogue gains more but loses Reputation, which opens access to Monasteries and Cities.
  • Spell, Advanced Action, Unit: taken from the Offer, for free. A free Unit still counts against your Command limit.
  • Artifact: draw 2, keep 1, as for a site reward.
  • “On top of the site reward”: the conquest pays out as usual, and the quest reward is added.
Reading a quest
🏠On site: at the quest's site
🗺️Elsewhere: you have to travel
Variant (expansion): if you play with the expansion named, the variant replaces the quest, or widens it when it says “also counts”. Without that expansion, ignore it.
Travel constraints
  • 🚶‍♀️ Escort: put a mana token on a free Command token. Otherwise a Ready Unit becomes the escort: it is spent and cannot be readied before you arrive. With no free slot and no Ready Unit, you cannot accept an escort quest.
  • 💎 Fragile item: put a crystal from the supply on your Hero. If a Wound reaches your hand before you arrive, the crystal is lost: the quest fails. On arrival, return it.
  • 🏃‍♀️ Race: put the token shown face down on the quest, and 1 crystal next to it at the end of each of your turns. On the 3rd, it attacks the nearest inhabited site (not this one). If you are there: combat, with your Units, the site is not fortified. Otherwise the quest fails and the site is exhausted.
  • 🎖️ Feat: the condition is added to the site's normal rules.
