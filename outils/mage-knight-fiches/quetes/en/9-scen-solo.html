Homemade scenarios

Rulebook solo setup: one Dummy player. You choose your Tactic first, it draws one at random, and both are removed for the rest of the game. Remove the four interactive Spells (no. 17 to 20) and your Hero's interactive Skill.

Quest module required, in Normal mode by default (module rules, pages 1 to 8). The City levels given already account for the quests: no +1.

The Knight Errant4 Rounds · 2 h

No City to take: you travel from village to monastery, one quest after another. The best way into the module.

Goal · complete 5 quests, at least one of them tier II.

Setup
  • Pyramid board.
  • 7 Countryside tiles: keep in priority those with a Village or Monastery.
  • 2 Core tiles without a City.
Scenario rules
  • Reminder: a Monastery only offers you quests if your Reputation gives you at least +1 Influence.

End · as soon as the 5th quest is completed, if one is tier II, play one last turn. Otherwise, at the end of the 4th Round.

The Hero's Path5 Rounds · 3 h

For a City to trust you with its missions, your name must be spotless. Win the people's trust, take the City, then prove yourself worthy of it.

Goal · complete a tier III quest without ever taking the rogue's path.

Setup
  • Pyramid board.
  • 8 Countryside tiles.
  • 1 Core tile with a City and 1 without.
  • The revealed City is level 4.
Scenario rules
  • Hero's path: you never choose 😈, never plunder a Village and never burn a Monastery. Otherwise, you lose the game.
  • Tier III: your Reputation must give you at least +2 Influence. Storming the City will cost you some: be ready.

End · as soon as the tier III quest is completed, play one last turn. Otherwise, at the end of the 5th Round.
